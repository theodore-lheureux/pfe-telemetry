---
title: The Recount app
date: 2026-11-03
---

Recount now has a web app where your team signs in and works with its telemetry.

- **Passwordless sign-in.** Sign in with a code sent to your email, or with a passkey.
- **Organizations and projects.** Group your work into projects. Projects are private, and only the people you add can see them.
- **Invitations and roles.** Invite teammates by email and choose what each person can do.
- **Collector credentials.** Create and revoke the credentials your machines use, right from project settings. Revoking one stops that machine from sending right away.
- **Saved moments.** Pick a machine and a time window, save it, and open it later to see what the machine was doing.
- **English and French.** The app is available in both languages.
- **A new look.** The app uses Recount's new design, in light and dark mode.

### Improvements

- Charts for long time windows load with less memory.
