---
title: The Recount app
date: 2026-11-03
---

Recount now has a web app where your team signs in and works with its telemetry.

### New

- **Passwordless sign-in.** Sign in with a code sent to your email, or with a passkey.
- **Organizations and projects.** Projects are private, and only the people you add can see them.
- **Invitations and roles.** Invite teammates by email and choose what each person can do.
- **Collector credentials.** Create and revoke machine credentials in project settings; a revoked machine stops sending right away.
- **Saved moments.** Save a machine and a time window, then open it later to see what the machine was doing.
- **English and French.** The app is available in both languages.

### Improved

- **A new look.** The app uses Recount's new design, in light and dark mode.
- **Faster charts.** Charts for long time windows load with less memory.
