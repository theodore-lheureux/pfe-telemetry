This policy explains what information Recount collects when you use Recount Cloud and this website, and what we do with it. If you self-host Recount, your data stays on your own servers and we never receive it.

## What we collect

- **Account information.** Your name and email address, used to sign you in and contact you about your account.
- **Telemetry you send.** The measurements your collectors send to your projects, and the moments you save.
- **Early access list.** If you ask to be notified, your email address and language, used only to tell you when Recount opens. Ask us at any time to remove it.
- **Technical logs.** Basic request logs, such as IP addresses and timestamps, kept to run the service and keep it secure.

## How we use it

We use this information only to provide, secure and improve Recount. We don't sell your information, and we don't use your telemetry for advertising.

## Who can see it

Your telemetry is visible only to the people you add to your projects. We share information with service providers that help us run Recount, such as hosting, only as needed to provide the service, or when the law requires it.

## Where it's stored

Recount Cloud stores your data in Canada.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Telemetry is kept until you delete it or close your account, after which it is removed from our systems.

## Cookies and local storage

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. This website remembers your theme and language choices in your browser, and doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.

The early access form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that submissions come from people. Turnstile looks at technical signals from your browser and doesn't use them for advertising.

We count visits to this website with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. It records pages viewed, referring sites, and general browser and country information.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information at any time. Contact us at [privacy@recount.cloud](mailto:privacy@recount.cloud).

## Changes

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we'll update the date above and let account holders know.
