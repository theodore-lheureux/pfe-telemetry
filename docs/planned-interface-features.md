The [telemetry analytics foundation](https://linear.app/pfe-telemetry/issue/PFE-73/build-telemetry-analytics-foundations-for-insights-and-dashboards) owns definition discovery, semantic queries, custom-query authoring, reusable analyses and dashboards. Explorer and Live expose only the capabilities their executors support. A definition appearing in a catalog does not make an absent Live payload field filterable.

The saved project week-start preference is available for future calendar controls under PFE-86. It does not change fixed-duration chart buckets or exact timestamps.

## Intentional restrictions

Project default access remains **No access** under the [access policy](application-access.md); that disabled field is not a pending public-project feature. Permission-based read-only views, loading/submission guards, pagination limits and unsupported-browser WebAuthn controls are also not missing implementations. The profile Gravatar controls, personal 2FA, passkeys, member CSV download, inspector search/copy and early-access notification form are implemented.
